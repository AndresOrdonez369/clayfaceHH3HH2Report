What each event counts

The six analytics events TeraCreators built into the Clayface event. The export gives daily totals, not unique players; goals reached and redemptions fire once per player, so those two equal players.

clayface_defeated
One player was in the room when Clayface was defeated. A three-player fight adds three.
clayface_clay_collected
One clay was picked up, including pickups after a player already has 20.
clayface_clay_goal_reached
One player reached 20 clays. Players who passed 20 before tracking started are not in it.
clayface_weapon_redeemed
One player claimed the Clay Goo Gun, from the banner or the weapon station.
clayface_dwell_rooms_5s
Five seconds spent by one player in a run room with Clayface assets while the event is live.
clayface_dwell_lobby_5s
Five seconds spent by one player in the main lobby or the Event Weapons hall while the event is live. Includes idle players standing there.
