# Clayface Weekend Analytics — surface brief

Scope: the weekend report page (index.html), delivered as a standalone HTML file and as the Claude Dashboard artifact page. Visitor mode: Operate.

Audience and job: the client team (Lobotommy) on Monday morning, confirming the Clayface event and clay system worked on Havoc Hotel 3 and Havoc Hotel 2 and checking any figure against its source file. Language: English. Avoid: generic SaaS look, childish game styling, dry spreadsheet.

Content: clay-system funnel per island (fights, clays, reached 20, redeemed, redemption rate), time with Clayface content (rooms vs lobby, by day), day-by-day table, island context (early quits over 30 days, last three weekends, queue), event definitions, sources and verification (ten Creator Portal CSVs, checks), open items (poster impressions not in export, unique players not in export).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each island is a collectible card and this page is its back, where the stat line lives. Refuses the KPI-tile grid where every number weighs the same.

OWN-WORLD: Coated card stock ground (#EEF0F2), ink navy shell (#14213D), HH3 band blue (#1D6FA3), HH2 band violet (#7A3FA8), Havoc yellow (#F2C230) only for the headline figure and clickable things, logo red (#D9381E) for not-measured states. Banded cards with 14px corners; condensed sports numerals (Big Shoulders Display) over Archivo text; every figure carries a fine-print catalog number (S1 to S10) to its file.

STORY: The client sees both islands' stat lines and the redemption rate in one glance, believes them because each cites its file, then scrolls to time, days, context and sources.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy shell bar with title, section tabs and the TeraCreators credit. Two island cards side by side: key art strip with band colour and version, four-cell stat line, redemption rate stamped largest in Havoc yellow, fine-print source line. Full-width navy "both islands" strip below. Signature move: the stat line; hovering any figure lights its catalog number and source.

FORM: Card Back Stat Line, candidate 3 of 7 on my list, seed 6587318b.

Raises: Saville catalog sleeve → catalog numbers on every figure. Variable-font specimen → hierarchy by scale alone. Warm consumer app → Havoc yellow reserved for headline and actions. Zoo guide map → one banded-card geometry frames art, stats and charts.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
